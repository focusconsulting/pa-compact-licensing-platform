# ATOM-GOV-ML-04

The PA Compact Model Legislation (CSG model act, enacted by participating states) states, at §2.P, L212:

> "For purposes of this Compact, any other title or status adopted by a State to replace the term 'physician assistant' shall be deemed synonymous with 'physician assistant' and shall confer the same rights and responsibilities to the Licensee under the provisions of this Compact at the time of its enactment."

This is a direct quote from the converted source file (`pa-compact-model-legislation.md`, L212); it is not a paraphrase.
