# ATOM-GOV-R3-04

PA Compact Rule 3, Compact Privilege, adopted 2026-04-06, states at 3.7(a)-(b) (PDF lines 233-247):

> "(a) An application for a qualifying license shall be considered opened from the date the application form is received by the state of qualifying license. (1) If the PA does not submit all requested materials, including any required fees, within 60 days after the application is opened, then the application shall be deemed incomplete and to have been withdrawn. (2) The PA must reapply and submit a new application and new nonrefundable application fees as determined by the state of qualifying license and the Compact Commission. (b) An application for a compact privilege shall be considered opened from the date the application form is received by the remote state(s). (1) If the PA does not submit all requested materials, including any required fees, within 60 days after the application is opened, then the application shall be deemed incomplete and to have been withdrawn. (2) The PA must reapply and submit a new application and new nonrefundable application fees as determined by the remote state(s) and the Compact Commission."

This is a direct quote from the adopted rule PDF.
