# ATOM-GOV-R3-02

PA Compact Rule 3, Compact Privilege, adopted 2026-04-06, states at 3.4(b) (PDF lines 138-147):

> "(b) When the state of qualifying license receives the application through the Compact Commission that state shall: (1) Evaluate the PA's eligibility for participating in the compact privilege process under the criteria set forth in Section 4 of the Compact Model Legislation; (2) Review the submitted criminal background check pursuant to Public Law 92-544 as required by the terms and provisions of the Compact; and (3) Issue notice, through the data system, to the Compact Commission verifying the PA's eligibility to participate in the Compact and confirming that the state will serve as the state of qualifying license or denying the PA's eligibility to participate in the Compact in accordance with Rule 3.9."

This is a direct quote from the adopted rule PDF.
