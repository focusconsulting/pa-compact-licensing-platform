# ATOM-GOV-ML-11

The PA Compact Model Legislation (CSG model act, enacted by participating states) states, at §6.G, L587–589:

> "If an Adverse Action is taken against a PA's Qualifying License, the PA's Compact Privilege in all Remote States shall be deactivated until two (2) years have elapsed after all restrictions have been removed from the State License. All disciplinary orders by the Participating State which issued the Qualifying License that impose Adverse Action against a PA's License shall include a Statement that the PA's Compact Privilege is deactivated in all Participating States during the pendency of the order."

This is a direct quote from the converted source file (`pa-compact-model-legislation.md`, L587–589); it is not a paraphrase.
