# ATOM-GOV-R5-06

The PA Compact draft Rule 5 "Data System" (renumbered Rule 4 on 2025-11-10; earlier draft text) states, at §5.5(b), L1401–1403:

> "All uniform data sets in the custody and control of the Compact Commission are confidential and exempt from public disclosure by the Compact Commission except for: (1) The names of participating PAs; and (2) The states where participating PAs hold Qualifying Licenses and Compact Privileges."

This is a direct quote from the converted source file (`pa-draft-rules-2_3_5.md`, L1401–1403); it is not a paraphrase.
