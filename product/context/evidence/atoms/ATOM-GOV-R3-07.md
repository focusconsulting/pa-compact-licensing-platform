# ATOM-GOV-R3-07

PA Compact Rule 3, Compact Privilege, adopted 2026-04-06, states at 3.5(a) (PDF lines 169-176):

> "(a) A compact privilege shall be valid until the expiration or revocation of the qualifying license used to apply for the privilege unless the privilege is terminated pursuant to an adverse action or the qualifying license is voluntarily terminated by the PA. The expiration date of the qualifying license shall be the expiration date that was in effect on the date the PA applied for the compact privilege. Any renewal of the qualifying license does not automatically renew the compact privilege. The PA must follow the procedure set forth in this Rule, in accordance with Section 4.A of the model legislation, in order to maintain any existing compact privilege(s)."

This is a direct quote from the adopted rule PDF.
