# ATOM-GOV-R5-01

The PA Compact draft Rule 5 "Data System" (renumbered Rule 4 on 2025-11-10; earlier draft text) states, at §5.2(g), L1138:

> "Participating states shall not disclose or report to the Compact Commission, and the Data System shall not receive or maintain, any criminal background check results obtained by Participating States."

This is a direct quote from the converted source file (`pa-draft-rules-2_3_5.md`, L1138); it is not a paraphrase.
