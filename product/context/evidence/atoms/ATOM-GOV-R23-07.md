# ATOM-GOV-R23-07

The PA Compact draft Rules 2 & 3 (redline approved by rules committee 2026-02-09) states, at Rule 3 §3.4(c), L486–489, 493:

> "Upon notice from the Compact Commission of eligibility approval by the state of qualifying license, to apply for a compact privilege the PA shall: (1) Complete the registration process established by the Compact Commission. (2) Identify the remote state(s) for which a compact privilege is requested. (3) … Follow the remote state(s) laws, regulations, and practice requirements and submit any proof of compliance requested by said remote state(s). … (4) Pay the non-refundable compact privilege fee required by the remote state(s) and any additional administrative fee required by the Compact Commission."

This is a direct quote from the converted source file (`pa-compact-rule-2-and-3-drafts.md`, L486–489, 493); it is not a paraphrase.
