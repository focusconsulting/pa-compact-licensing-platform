# ATOM-GOV-R4-01

PA Compact Rule 4, Compact Data System, Confidentiality and Information Sharing, adopted 2026-04-06, states at 4.3(c) (PDF lines 115-134):

> "As a State of Qualifying License, a Participating State shall verify and submit the following information for each PA applying for or holding a Qualifying License: (1) Full legal name; (2) Other name(s) used, previously or currently; (3) Sex; (4) Date of birth; (5) Social security number; (6) Primary residence address of record; (7) Telephone number of record; (8) E-mail address designated by applicant to receive correspondence from the Compact Commission and Participating States; (9) PA educational program completed, including year of graduation; (10) NCCPA Certification number, current certification status and certification expiration date; (11) License number, status, issue date and expiration date in the designated State of Qualifying License; (12) Adverse actions against a License or Compact Privilege; (13) The existence of Significant Investigative Information; and (14) Any denial of licensure, and the reason(s) for such denial (excluding the reporting of any criminal history record information where prohibited by law)."

This is a direct quote from the adopted rule PDF.
