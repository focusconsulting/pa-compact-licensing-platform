# ATOM-GOV-M0825-02

The PA Compact Commission Rules Committee minutes, 2025-08-25 (amended, approved) record an exchange at L268–271 on who is responsible for a PA's address in the uniform data set. T. Terranova proposed that the state verify it; Chair Loucka noted the SQL may not hold the current address; J. Alley described the IMLC practice in which the PA updates the address in the system, the update populates to member states, and the SQL follows up if it has concerns; Chair Loucka accepted that this accounts for the discrepancy.

> "§ T. Terranova: Draft rule 3 says it's the PA's responsibility to provide the commission with their address. I think the state should be responsible for verifying that information. § Chair Loucka: Regarding the address, if people are moving around a lot since the compact does not require a home state, the state of qualifying license may not have the most up-to-date address for practitioners. § J. Alley: My experience from the IMLC, we routinely get applications in which the address the physician provides is not what is on file, so we regularly verify addresses. If a PA comes to the system to update their address, that would be populated to all the member states. If the state of qualifying license had concerns, they could follow up with the PA. § Chair Loucka: I think it does account for the discrepancy when practitioners do not keep their information up to date with their state licensing board."
