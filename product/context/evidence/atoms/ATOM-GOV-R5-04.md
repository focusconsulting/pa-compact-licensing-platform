# ATOM-GOV-R5-04

The PA Compact draft Rule 5 "Data System" (renumbered Rule 4 on 2025-11-10; earlier draft text) states, at §5.3(e), L1231–1236:

> "The uniform data set shall also include the following additional information: (1) All primary residence address changes provided by the participating PA to the Compact Commission, and the date the address was changed; (2) All e-mail address changes provided by the participating PA to the Compact Commission, and the date the e-mail address was changed; (3) All applications, attestations or certifications submitted by the participating PA …; (4) All documents submitted by a Participating State to verify or decline a PA's eligibility for Compact participation or to issue a Compact Privilege; and (5) Evidence of the current certification status with the NCCPA for all PAs holding Compact privileges."

This is a direct quote from the converted source file (`pa-draft-rules-2_3_5.md`, L1231–1236); it is not a paraphrase.
