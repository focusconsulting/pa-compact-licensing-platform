# ATOM-GOV-R4-03

PA Compact Rule 4, Compact Data System, Confidentiality and Information Sharing, adopted 2026-04-06, states at 4.3(d) (PDF lines 135-141):

> "(d) As a Remote State, a Participating State shall verify and submit, where applicable, the following information for each PA applying for or holding a Compact Privilege in the Remote State: (1) Compact Privilege issue date, status, expiration date and privilege number or other unique privilege identifier issued by the remote state; (2) Adverse actions against a Compact Privilege; and (3) The existence of Significant Investigative Information."

This is a direct quote from the adopted rule PDF.
