# ATOM-GOV-RFP-01

The PA Compact Commission Data System RFP (Statement of Objectives, October 2025) states, at §3.1 Priority User Stories, L54:

> "As a physician assistant, I want to be able to go online, apply for the interstate privilege to practice, pay the fees, monitor the status of my privilege requests, receive notification of any requirements to complete the qualifying process, and get a confirmation once my practice privilege has been issued so I can get authorization to practice in other states efficiently."
