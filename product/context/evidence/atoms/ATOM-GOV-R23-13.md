# ATOM-GOV-R23-13

The PA Compact draft Rules 2 & 3 (redline approved by rules committee 2026-02-09) states, at Rule 3 §3.6, L680:

> "If the PA does not submit all requested materials, including any required fees, within 60 days after the application is opened, then the application shall be deemed to have been withdrawn. … The PA must reapply and submit a new application and new nonrefundable application fees"
