# ATOM-GOV-R23-01

The PA Compact draft Rules 2 & 3 (redline approved by rules committee 2026-02-09) states, at Rule 2 §2.1(a), L214–222:

> "The PA shall designate a Participating State as the state of qualifying license for purposes of registration for a compact privilege through the Compact if the PA possesses a full and unrestricted license to conduct medical services in that state, and the state is: (1) The state of primary residence for the PA, or (2) The state where at least twenty-five percent of the active medical services occurs, or (3) The location of the PA's current employer, or (4) If no state qualifies under subparagraph (1), subparagraph (2), or subparagraph (3), the state designated as state of residence for purposes of federal income tax. (5) A service member, or the service member's spouse, may retain their state of primary residence designation during the period the service member is on active duty."

This is a direct quote from the converted source file (`pa-compact-rule-2-and-3-drafts.md`, L214–222); it is not a paraphrase.
