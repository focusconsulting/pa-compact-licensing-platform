## Call to order/Roll call

Tim Terranova, appointed by L. Marx and acting as chair in Chair Marx's absence, calls the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. ET.

- A. Mortell calls roll. 3/6 voting members present. Committee does not have quorum.
- Chair Terranova announces that in absence of quorum no business will be conducted, no votes will be taken, but information will be taken in by those present.

## FY26 Quarterly Budget Review

- A. Mortell provides an overview of the PA Compact Commission's quarterly budget report, which reflects expenditures from the first quarter of FY26. The FY25 expenditures have been updated with actualized costs, some of which were estimations when the committee last reviewed the budget in September 2025.
- o G. Thomas asks if the FY25 numbers are final for the whole fiscal year.
- o A. Mortell confirms.
- o C. Sims points out that the FY26 remaining revenue for AAPA and NCCA are inclusive of the request funds from both of those organizations, which would extend past the current funding agreement that ends in March 2026. Also, during August and September, there were charges to the commission general funds as the new HRSA grant was being put in place, and now expenditures will be charged to the HRSA funds until those funds are expended.
- o No further questions or comments from the committee.

## 2026 Meeting Plan

- Chair Terranova reminds the committee that all committees are to establish a routine meeting schedule for 2026. It is Chair Marx's inclination to set a regular quarterly meeting, with any additional meetings taking place as needed, along with monthly meetings between Chair Marx, C. Sims, and A. Mortell to review the budget and determine if any additional meetings were necessary.

## Delegate Questions and Comments

## None. Public Questions and Comments None.

## Adjourn

- With no further business or discussion, the meeting ends at 1:20 p.m. ET.
