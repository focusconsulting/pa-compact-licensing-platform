## What is the PA Licensure Compact?

vides state

The PA Licensure Compact is an interstate occupational licensure compact for physician assistants/associates (PAs). The compact provides a streamlined process via a compact privilege for PAs to practice in other compact member states without the need to obtain multiple state licenses. States join the compact by legislatively enacting the model compact legislation.

## What You Need to Know

<!-- image -->

- The model compact legislation has been finalized and is available for state adoption.
- Qualifying PAs in a compact member state who meet all compact requirements may practice in other compact member states via a compact privilege, which is considered to be equivalent to a license.
- A PA will utilize the data system to obtain individual compact privileges for any participating state they wish to practice.
- The PA Licensure Compact Commission was activated in September 2024 after seven states adopted the model language. Historically, the process for a licensure compact to become fully operational can take up to 24 months after its activation.

## Compact Benefits

- Increases collaboration among compact member states
- Maintains public safety and reduces application time through a shared data system
- Facilitates multistate practice by reducing the burden of maintaining multiple licenses
- Improves access to PA services and the use of telehealth
- Supports relocating practicing PA military spouses
- Improves continuity of care when patients or PAs relocate
- Expands employment opportunities for PAs into new markets
- Preserves PA state-based scope of practice
