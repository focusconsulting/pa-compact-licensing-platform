## Legislative Update

- A. Mortell provides legislative update:
- o Compact currently has 24 member states.
- o MO HB 2974 has passed both chambers. The governor has until July to sign legislation.
- o PA Compact legislation filed in eight other states-AK, IL, MA, MI, NH, NY, PA, and RI.
- § Compact amended to HB 110 in Alaska, session ends May 20, 2026
- § IL session ends May 31, 2026
- § Sessions in other states end in June, July, November and December. Staff will continue to monitor remaining legislation for this session.

## Committee Updates

- Other committees have not met since the last Executive Committee meeting.
- Rules Committee will meet June 8, 2026.
- Finance Committee will meet June 9, 2026.
- Communications Committee will meet June 4, 2026.

## AAPA Board of Directors Meeting
