## FY27 Draft Budget Review

- Chair Marx provides an overview of the FY27 draft budget.
- o A. Mortell notes costs for FY26 are currently estimated based on the current monthly spending average, as costs through end of FY26 (September 2026) have not been fully realized.
- FY27 Budget Line Item -Secretariat Services

- o A. Mortell notes the budget range of $136,500-$300,000 has been provided to the committee for its consideration, and it was staff's intention in preparing the budget this way that this committee decide on a specific number to recommend to the Executive Committee. $136,500 is a 5% increase from FY26 budget for Secretariat Services, while $300,000 is based on the budgets set by other compact commissions (OT Compact and Counseling Compact) that have transitioned to permanent secretariats, which usually have higher rates.
- o Given the anticipated increase in workload for commission staff and legal services for data system development and system launch, the committee discusses setting budget at $200,000. The Commission does not currently intend to transition to a permanent secretariat during FY27, so the committee does not see it necessary to budget at the highest end of the range provided.
- o Chair Marx requests a motion to set the Secretariat Services budget to $200,000.

## Motion :

- o Sandra Harrison motions to set $200,000 as the recommendation to Executive Committee for Secretariat Services in FY27.
- o Jamie Alley seconds.
- o All members present voted in favor; none abstained; motion passed.
- FY27 Budget Line Item -Legal Services
- o A. Mortell explains commission staff has provided a budget range of $44,000-$75,000 for the committee's consideration . $44,000 is a 10% increase from FY26 based on the Commission's expectation of spending $37,000 of the $40,000 budgeted for FY26. The upper range of $75,000 is based on the OT Compact Commission's FY25 recorded costs and the Counseling Compact Commission's FY25 budget.
- o N. Kalfas advises the committee to anticipate increased legal costs due to the work commission staff provides during state legislative sessions and the workload anticipated for data system development.
- o Chair Marx requests a motion to set the Legal Services budget to $75,000.

## Motion :

- o Rob Patchett motions to set $75,000 as the recommendation to Executive Committee for Legal Services in FY27.
- o Vonda Apking seconds.
- o All members present voted in favor; none abstained; motion passed.
- FY27 Budget Line Items -IT Contractual Services &amp; Database Maintenance and Hosting
- o Chair Marx notes for IT Contractual Services that $270,000 is the anticipated cost for FY27.
- o Chair Marx notes the cost of data system maintenance and hosting is variable as the system has not yet been built.
- o Committee staff note the range of $50,000-$70,000 was provided based on the costs other commissions have seen to maintain and host their data systems.
- o N. Kalfas notes a budget for cyber security should be added, as that will be a need for the Commission once the system is built.

## Commission Privilege Fee

- Chair Marx opens discussion on Commission's compact privilege fee.
- A. Mortell updates the committee that commission staff have verified the data on PAs with multiple licenses, pursuant to the discussion during the last committee meeting. Based on 2024 data, which is the most recent, 12% of licensees hold two licenses and 4% hold 3 or more. These are the figures the committee had during the last meeting and continue to be the most up to date available.
- o J. Kolb asks why the projections of anticipated revenue based on varying levels of PA participation jump between 5, 10, 15, and 20% profession participation.
- o A. Mortell notes these calculations were provided for the committee's consideration by staff, and additional data points can be added if useful for the discussion. Among other compacts, 20% profession participation is considered the maximum a commission could anticipate reaching, and it would be reached over time rather than within the first year of compact activation.
- Commission staff will work to get cost estimates to add a cyber security budget line item ahead of Executive Committee's budget review.
- o The committee discusses setting a placeholder figure for maintenance and hosting that may be adjusted based on data system development and discussions with the data system vendor.
- J. Kolb asks if maintenance and hosting costs are for after the system is built, separate from the $270,000 for database development, and if it would be a yearly recurring cost spread out monthly.
- A. Mortell confirms the maintenance and hosting budget is for costs associated with updating code, Cloud data storage, etc. for the system after it is built. It would be a yearly cost consideration.
- J. Kolb asks if these costs are anticipated later in FY27 rather than occurring immediately.
- A. Mortell confirms these costs would begin to accrue after the system has been built, which would be several months into FY27.
- J. Kolb notes the budget for maintenance and hosting could be prorated until the committee and Commission has more information to make an estimate, since they are not costs that would be actualized until several months into FY27.
- o Chair Marx recommends setting a placeholder of at least $50,000 for the Maintenance and Hosting budget, the low end of the budget range considered, since it is not knowable at this time, but costs are anticipated in this area.
- o Chair Marx requests a motion to transfer the FY27 draft budget recommendations to the Executive Committee as discussed during today's meeting.
