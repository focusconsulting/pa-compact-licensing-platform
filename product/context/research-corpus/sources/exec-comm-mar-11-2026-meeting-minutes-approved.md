## Legislative Update

- Alabama compact legislation delivered to the governor and awaits signature.
- PA Compact legislation filed in eleven other states -AK, FL, GA, HI, IL, MA, MI, MO, NH, NY, and PA.
- o GA, MI, NH, and MA bills have been transmitted to the second chamber.
- o AK, HI, IL, MA, MO, and NY bills remain in houses of origin.
- o Filed in FL but unlikely to move before the end of session.

## Clean Slate Laws

- Chair Terranova provides an overview of the development of Clean Slate Laws proposed in several states, so the compact commission may be aware of it and anticipate issues that may arise as a result.
- o N. Kalfas notes this may be an issue for the compliance committee, and it may also require a rule or policy to be generated that reminds member states of the need to comply with the compact, which supersedes conflicting state laws. Recommends the commission find out what states have this kind of law.

## Website Domain Migration Update

- A. Mortell updates the committee that FSMB's IT staff are working with members of the OT Compact Commission's IT team to determine who needs to be listed on the .gov migration application. A. Mortell will submit the application as current interim executive director for the PA Compact Commission. Applications are not currently being processed due to the government shut down, so commission staff will take steps to prepare the application in the meantime.

## Full Commission Meeting Notice

## Technology Committee

- Chair Terranova explains the need to create a technology committee to oversee data system development. The commission is empowered to create ad hoc committees. Chair is willing to open the committee up to subject experts and IT staff from states who have technical knowledge. Chair requests the committee to consider if they know people who would be suitable to serve on the committee and send nominations to A. Mortell.
- o E. Huntley asks what the timeline is and notes a desire to involve Minnesota's IT staff who have been involved with the IMLCC.
- o Chair Terranova explains if the goal of putting a contract in place by the end of this month is met, then system development would occur from May -September of this year, according to the timeline in the vendor's proposal.
- o N. Kalfas suggests the committee vote to delegate certain responsibilities to the technology committee. The committee will also need state-appointed delegates to vote on items during their meetings.
- Chair Terranova agree s that can be a voting item for next month's meeting.
- o K. Scarbalis asks if the Commission will need to request additional funding at this point in anticipation of data system maintenance.
- Chair Terranova notes the data system contract negotiations will inform any additional funding requests.
