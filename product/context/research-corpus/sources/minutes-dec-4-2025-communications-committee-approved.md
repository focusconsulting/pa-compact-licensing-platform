## Review of Commonly Asked Questions

- Chair Fischer asks A. Mortell for an overview of the FAQs under review and how often questions are sent to the PA Compact email.
- o A. Mortell reports it averages one email a week with questions often repeating, particularly those asking how to apply for a compact privilege. The FAQ sheet provided to the committee for review today is comprised of the following questions:
- i. What steps should PAs interested in the compact complete now?
- ii. Is the application for a compact privilege open and available yet? Can I apply for a compact privilege?
- iii. Is the application for a compact privilege open and available yet? Can I apply for a compact privilege?
- iv. Does a misdemeanor conviction render a PA ineligible for a compact privilege?
