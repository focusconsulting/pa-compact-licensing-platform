## Legislative Update

- A. Mortell provides legislative update:
- o The PA Compact currently has 29 compact member states. 10 states have joined so far during the 2026 legislative session.
- o Legislation is filed in two states -MA and PA.
- Chair Terranova notes new member states are contacted by commission staff soon after compact legislation is enacted to initiate the Commissioner appointment and onboarding process.

## Committee Updates

- Communications -committee has not met since last Executive Committee meeting. The committee will meet next week to draft the September newsletter.
- Finance -Chair Terranova requests that Finance Committee updates be held until FY27 budget discussion item.
- Rules -committee met Monday, August 10, 2026, and moved four policies to the Executive Committee for consideration. The Executive Committee will review these policies during its next meeting.

## Data System Contract and Funding MOU Update

- Chair Terranova updates the committee that Focus has agreed to share the draft data system Statement of Work/contract with the Commission's funding partners excepting one section. A non-disclosure agreement has been circulated, which must be signed prior to the data system contract being shared. The MOU should be signed soon after the data system contract is received by the funders. The current data system contract contemplates development of an MVP, so further contract negotiations will be necessary in the future to facilitate state onboarding and system maintenance.

## Draft Rule 8 -Records Exempt from Disclosure

- Chair Terranova opens discussion on draft Rule 8 -Records Exempt from Disclosure.
- Chair Terranova provides an overview of the rule, explaining the concept is similar to rules drafted for the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact, though the content is not identical.

## Motion:

## Draft Rule 2 -Definitions

- Chair Terranova opens discussion on draft Rule 2 -Definitions.
- Chair Terranova explains that a rule on definitions has become common practice among compact commissions, so anyone can see all definitions used in rules without pulling up each individual rule.
- o No questions or comments from committee.
- Chair Terranova requests a motion to move draft Rule 2 to public comment.

## Motion:

- Marisa Courtney motions to release draft Rule 2 -Definitions for public comment.
- Larry Marx seconds motion.
- All members present voted in favor; none abstained; motion passed.

## Delegate and Public Questions and Comments

- J. Kolb asks for information on the public comment process and what outreach is done to garner comments.
- o N. Kalfas notes notice of rules available for public comment is posted on the PA Compact website.
- o A. Mortell notes items released for public comment are sent to Commissioners from all Member States. The Commission does not currently have a list of people who have requested to receive notice of Commission rule making and public comment periods.
- o Chair Terranova notes items released for public comment are shared with the ex-officios from partner organizations, which further spreads the request for public comments.
- J. Kolb reiterates the Academy Association for Physician Associates' commitment to the work of the Commission, though the money the Commission has budgeted for is not necessarily guaranteed.
- o Chair Terranova notes these considerations are appreciated and were noted by the Finance Committee when shared during their last meeting.
