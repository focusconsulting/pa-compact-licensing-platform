## Review Rules of Finance Committee

- N. Kalfas gives a brief overview of Finance Committee processes.
- Budget is a proposed draft the initial year
- Proposed draft budget gives a general idea of what commission needs to function
- Build draft, then send to executive committee, then it will be sent to the full committee

## Funding Update

- J. Thomas shares that there aren't any official updates, conversations are ongoing.
- G. Thomas shares that it's likely that the initial funding will be from NCCPA and AAPA. NCCPA is having discussions at the board level, meeting in February. NCCPA is prepared to approve $150,000.
- K. Scarbalis shares that AAPA is meeting in March. They'll have more information in early 2025.

Question- How much HRSA is left for this project?

$50,000 this year, $50,000 next year. This is not on a calendar year. This year's HRSA money is expected to last through February.

## Draft Budget

- L. Marx explains draft budget. Discusses placeholders.
- N. Kalfas gives brief description of what other compacts have done for a secretariat and ED.
- For full secretariat services, no less than $120,000 annually.
- For minimal services such as bank account and bookkeeping, $25,000 annually.
- In-kind secretariat services from an affiliated organization such as PT and Nursing.

Questions posed regarding the draft budget

Can CSG continue services on a temporary basis? Yes, CSG will get information about costs.
