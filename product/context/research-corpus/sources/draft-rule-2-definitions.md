## 2.0 Purpose:

Pursuant to Section 7 and for the purpose of the rules adopted by the PA Compact Commission, the following definitions shall apply. Terms not specifically defined in these Rules shall have the definition as set forth in the Compact. In the event of a conflict with definitions found elsewhere in these Rules, definitions found in Section 2.1 shall control and prevail.

## 2.1 Definitions:

- (a) 'Administrative fee' or 'service fee' means fees that may be assessed by the PA Licensure Compact Commission, member state, or both, to handle and process an application to select a state of qualifying license, or a commission compact privilege fee
- authorized by Section 7.E of the model compact language. An administrative or service fee is separate from the privilege fee established by participating states. (b) "Adverse Action" means any administrative, civil, equitable, or criminal action permitted by a State's laws which is imposed by a Licensing Board or other authority against a PA License or License application or Compact Privilege such as License denial, censure, revocation, suspension, probation, monitoring of the Licensee, or restriction on the Licensee's practice. (c) 'Compact' means the PA Licensure Compact. (d) "Compact Privilege" means the authorization granted by a Remote State to allow a Licensee from another Participating State to practice as a PA to provide Medical Services and other licensed activity to a patient located in the Remote State under the Remote State's laws and regulations. (e) 'Compact Privilege fee' or 'privilege fee' means the fee a participating state establishes for a privilege issued through the compact. means all information and documents required to be shared in compliance with the Compact shall be transmitted confidentially and may not be
- (f) 'Confidential and filed under seal' discoverable in civil litigation, re-disclosed voluntarily or pursuant to a public records
