- Jamie Alley seconds.

## Review and Adopt December 8, 2025, meeting minutes

Committee reviews the minutes from December 8, 2025. Minutes could not be adopted at the last meeting as the committee lacked quorum.

- J. Alley notes a necessary amendment to a spelling error under the minute's subheading, 'FY26 Quarterly Budget Review Chair,' where 'first' in 'first quarter' is misspelled.
- Chair Marx calls for a motion to adopt the minutes as amended.

## Motion :

- Jamie Alley motions to adopt the December 8, 2025, minutes as amended.
- Vonda Apking seconds.
- All members present voted in favor; none abstained; motion passed.

## FY26 March Quarterly Budget Review

- A. Mortell provides an overview of the PA Compact Commission's quarterly budget report, which reflects expenditures from October 2025 through January 2026. The budget report now includes a 'Percentage Budget Spent to Date' column for the committee's reference.
- o Chair Marx notes the commission may see an increase in secretariat and legal fees in the coming months once the data system vendor contract is put in place, and data system development begins.
- o N. Kalfas notes that other commissions have needed additional outside legal counsel during their data system development, but this commission will hopefully not need those services given the legal experience present among the commissioners. This would assist with managing commission costs.

## Delegate Questions and Comments

- Chair Terranova thanks the committee for the thoughtful discussion and encourages the committee to take the time needed to determine the fee.
- G. Thomas notes the data sheet from the meeting materials shows commission fees for other compacts are under $100, which would indicate the PA Compact Commission can set a fee at a similar level.
