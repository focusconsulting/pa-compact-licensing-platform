## Legislative Update

- A. Mortell provides legislative update:
- o Legislation enacted in Alabama for a current total of 24 compact member states.
- o PA Compact legislation filed in nine other states -AK, IL, MA, MI, MO, NH, NY, PA, and RI.
- MI and NH bills have been transmitted to the second chamber.
- AK, IL, MA, MO, and NY bills remain in houses of origin.
- PA Compact filed in RI as governor's budget amendment.
- HI bill will not progress this session. CSG will meet with governor's office policy unit to address changes needed before next legislative session.
- GA session ended before House could concur on bill. Legislation expected to be filed again next year.
- FL session ended and bill did not pass.

## Committee Updates

- Other committees have not met since the last Executive Committee meeting.
- Rules Committee will meet Monday, April 20, 2026.
- Finance Committee will meet June 9, 2026.
- Communications Committee will meet June 4, 2026. Additional meeting before June is in the process of being scheduled.

- o Rules will be posted on the website pending final proofing by A. Mortell and N. Kalfas.
- o E. Huntley and M. Courtney note the meeting was efficiently run.

## Motion:

- Jamie Alley motions that the Technology Committee be delegated the authority to complete two-week interval approvals of the data system development process and report back to the Executive Committee monthly.
- Marisa Courtney seconds motion.
- All members present voted in favor; none abstained; motion passed.

## NCCPA/AAPA MOU

- Chair Terranova updates the committee that commission chair and staff received a revised version of the MOU from NCCPA and AAPA. Commission chair and staff have scheduled a meeting for next week to meet with leadership in those organizations to discuss the changes with the goal of getting an MOU in place.
- o K. Scarbalis asks if funding for the compact would be available through the Rural Health Transformation Program.
- o Chair Terranova notes that would likely be a state-specific question, and most states have created blueprints for how the funding they receive will be used.
- o G. Thomas notes that NCCPA's first payment under the MOU, though not signed, has been sent. Other funding options may be explored once the Commission has more information.
- o J. Alley agrees that other funding options can be explored when the Commission knows they are needed.

## Data System Contract Update

- Chair Terranova updates the committee that a draft contract has been received from Focus and is being reviewed by commission chair and staff.
- o J. Alley asks if signing of the contract will be contingent upon resolving any issues surrounding the funding MOU.
- o Chair Terranova notes Rules Chair Loucka provided great assistance during the meeting with a rules overview.

## Technology Committee Appointments and Delegation of Responsibilities to Technology Committee

- Chair Terranova notes the Commission's intention to create an ad hoc Technology Committee. Chair intends to empower the Technology Committee to approve work completed by data system vendor, Focus, through biweekly sprint reviews.
- o K. Scarbalis asks if commissioners have volunteered to join the committee.
- o Chair Terranova confirms that nominations for commissioners have been received, but additional members are needed.
- o A. Mortell will send out a reminder email to all commissioners and alternates requesting volunteer signups per J. Hepner's suggestion.
- o J. Alley asks if the scope of the delegation of responsibility is approving the track of work and not renegotiating the contract.
- Chair Terranova confirms the contract will be signed and not able to be amended by the Technology Committee.

## Draft Rule 5 -Joint Investigations

## Draft Rule 6 -Fees

- Chair Terranova opens discussion on draft rule 6 -Fees and explains that the committee will review it for compatibility with the data system as it is being developed and may hold the rule in this committee until the Co mmission's fees have been set. This committee can also send the rule back to the Rules Committee for redrafting if needed.
- o J. Hepner asks if the rule needs to specify that the Commission may change the fees.
- o Chair Terranova and L. Monick note since the fees are set in rule, the process for changing the fees would be same as amending a rule per the rule on rulemaking, so it need not be stipulated in the fee rule.
- No further questions or edits from committee. The rule remains in Executive Committee pending data system development and the Finance Committee's determination of commission privilege fees.

## Delegate and Public Questions and Comments

- o Chair Terranova confirms that information is tracked by commission staff and can be provided.
