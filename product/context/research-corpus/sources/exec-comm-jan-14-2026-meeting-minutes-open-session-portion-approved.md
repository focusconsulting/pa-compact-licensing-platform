## Statement on Conflicts of Interest

- Chair Terranova shares the following statement on conflicts of interest:
- o 'Many commissioners may be concerned that they have a conflict of interest due to knowledge of or experience with one or more of the vendors who have responded to the RFP for the data system. After conferring with legal counsel, I wanted to remind commissioners that it is possible to have a potential conflict that is waiveable. The appropriate course of action is to disclose the potential conflict and then use your judgment to determine whether or not that potential conflict would prohibit your ability to make a decision that is solely in the best interest of the PA Compact Commission. If you believe you are able to make a decision with only the PA Compact Commission's authority and goals in mind, the potential conflict can be waived.
