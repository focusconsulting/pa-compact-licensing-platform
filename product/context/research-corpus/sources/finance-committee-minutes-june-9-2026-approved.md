## Welcome

## Call to order/Roll call

Chair Marx calls the meeting to order at 2:05 p.m. ET.

A. Mortell calls roll. 6/8 voting members present.

## Review and Adopt Agenda

Committee reviews the agenda; Chair Marx calls for a motion to adopt the agenda.

## Motion :

- Elizabeth Huntley motions to adopt the agenda.
- Amy Embry seconds.
- All members present voted in favor; none abstained; motion passed.

## Review and Adopt March 10, 2026, meeting minutes

Committee reviews the minutes from March 10, 2026; Chair Marx calls for a motion to adopt the minutes as drafted.

## Motion :

- Jamie Alley motions to adopt the March 10, 2026, minutes.
- Elizabeth Huntley seconds.
- All members present voted in favor; none abstained; motion passed.

## FY27 MOU Update

- Jennifer Kolb, AAPA Chief Medical Officer and Interim Ex-Officio, updates the committee that AAPA is updating the funding MOU between the Commission, AAPA, and NCCPA, and it will be shared with the parties in the next week or so for review and signing.

## FY26 June Quarterly Budget Review

- Chair Marx provides an overview of the budget, which covers seven months of expenditure from October 2025 to April 2026. Expenditures include secretariat and legal, with secretariat reporting a 46% budget spent to date and legal reporting 54%.
- o No questions from committee.

## Commission Privilege Fee

- Chair Marx opens discussion on Commission's privilege fee and the data provided by commission staff as part of meeting materials.
- C. Sims notes FSMB provided the following numbers on current PAs who hold multiple licenses: 12% hold two licenses; 4% hold three or more licenses.

- o G. Thomas notes these numbers may have been updated. Commission staff to follow up with FSMB and NCCPA regarding more recent numbers.
- o J. Kolb shares that AAPA may provide additional data that would be directive to the committee in determining a fee, particularly in relation to estimating the number of privileges the Commission is likely to issue.
- N. Kalfas notes other commissions have discussed charging a commission fee per privilege v. a single commission fee when multiple privileges are bought simultaneously. The latter method poses issues for a commission in accurately recording the number of privileges issued and budgeting for future years based on those numbers.
- o Committee is in favor of charging a commission fee per privilege, which is in line with other commissions.

## Delegate Questions and Comments

- Chair Terranova informs the committee that approving the FY27 budget during an August 2026 Full Commission meeting would require approving the draft FY27 budget in this committee in July.
- Chair Marx requests the committee meet again July 7 at 2pm ET to review and approve the FY27 budget for transmission to the Executive Committee.
- o No objections from committee.

## Public Questions and Comments None.

## Adjourn

- Hearing no further business or discussion, Chair Marx adjourns meeting at 2:57 p.m. ET.
