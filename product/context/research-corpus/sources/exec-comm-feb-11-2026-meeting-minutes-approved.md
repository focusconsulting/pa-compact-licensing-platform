## Legislative Update

A. Mortell gives a legislative update. Active legislation can be found on the compact map on the PA Website: https://www.pacompact.org/#compact.

- Compact was enacted in North Dakota with substantive changes on 1/28/26. Commission staff and legal counsel will meet with appointment delegate and ND board to ensure the state's participation adheres to the requirements of the Compact until the enacting legislation can be amended during the next legislative session. With New Jersey's enactment in January, the PA Compact now has 21 Member States.
- Legislation is filed in 12 states -AL, AZ, FL, HI, IL, MA, MI, MO, NM, NY, PA, and SD.
- Legislation is anticipated in GA and RI but has not yet been filed.
- AL HB 156 has crossed over to the Senate. NM HB 10 has also crossed over to the Senate, though commission staff and legal counsel are working on addressing substantive changes in the bill.
- MI HB 4309 and PA HB 1961, both carried over from 2025, are under consideration in their respective state Senates.
- Most other filed legislation has received committee assignments in their house of origin.
