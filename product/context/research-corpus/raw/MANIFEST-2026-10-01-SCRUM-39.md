# Commission records added 2026-10-01 (SCRUM-39)

These are the Commission's records from March to September 2026, including the adopted Rules 3 and 4. Before this, the corpus ended at March 2026.

How each file was checked:

- Each PDF was downloaded from pacompact.org on 2026-10-01 and compared byte for byte (SHA-1) with the copy here. Four of them (bylaws, code of conduct, brochure, transition timeline) were first downloaded on 2026-05-13. They were compared again on 2026-10-01 and still match.
- The text versions were made with docling 2.69.1, using `convert.py` from the research-corpus-mcp bundle. Their front matter uses the same fields as the existing files in `sources/`, plus a `corrections:` field on Rule 3.
- Rules 3 and 4 were compared word by word with their PDFs. Docling had dropped one line from Rule 3.1(a), the definition of Adverse Action (PDF line 23). It was put back by hand, and the `corrections:` field at the top of that file says so.
- The other 59 text versions were not compared with their PDFs. Check the PDF before quoting from one.

Not in the repo:
